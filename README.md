Starlight Garden is a 2D cozy magical adventure game. The idea of Starlight Garden is the player plays as a magical flower princess that is on a mission
to restore a grand Celestial Garden after a shower of falling stars is left it in darkness. The starlight and magical energy has fallen into a Japanese-inspired
garden landscape filled with various flowers: mostly including Sakura Trees, Asagao (Morning Glory), Platycodon (Balloon Flower), Star Lily (Lily Stargazer). Star 
Petals and Sakura Blossoms must be collected while avoiding shadowy mischievous spirits that like the darkness. These are what power and activate the Crystal
Blooms to bring light back to the graden while keeping harmony and health.
